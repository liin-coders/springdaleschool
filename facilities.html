Campus & Co-Curricular Life

Modern Infrastructure Built for Discovery, Safety & Growth

Every corner of our Changran, Kathua campus is designed to spark curiosity—from high-tech STEM laboratories to championship sports arenas and creative clubs.

Flagship Innovation Hub

NITI Aayog Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL)

Spring Dales English School is proudly recognized as an Atal Tinkering Lab School under NITI Aayog’s Atal Innovation Mission. Our ATL is a dedicated workspace where young minds explore electronics, robotics, 3D printing, and sensor-based engineering.

  • Hands-on DIY kits: Arduino, Raspberry Pi, IoT modules & mechanical tools
  • Guided mentorship for INSPIRE Awards, National Science Fairs & ATL Marathons
  • Problem-solving projects addressing water conservation, agriculture & smart safety
